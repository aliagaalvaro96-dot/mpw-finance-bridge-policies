Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

MPW Codex Finance Bridge is a private internal tool operated by Mister Pressure Wash LLC (MPW). It compares completed Jobber work with QuickBooks Online records for reconciliation.

Information we process

With authorization, the bridge may process company information, customer names, invoice numbers, invoice dates, invoice amounts, transaction-status information, Jobber job numbers, completion dates, client names, visit values, and related operational information.

Use, security, and sharing

We use this information only to identify reconciliation exceptions. We do not use it for advertising, sale, profiling, or unrelated purposes. OAuth tokens and protected reconciliation reports are encrypted. Access is limited to authorized MPW personnel and providers needed to operate the bridge, including Intuit, Jobber, and the cloud host. MPW does not sell this information.

No financial changes

The bridge is designed for read-only accounting queries. It does not create, edit, send, void, delete, pay, or collect on QuickBooks transactions. Jobber billing reminders and invoice statuses do not trigger billing or collection actions.

For privacy questions or a deletion request, contact info@misterpressurewash.com.

Terms of Use

Effective August 13, 2026

These terms govern MPW Codex Finance Bridge, a private internal reconciliation tool operated by Mister Pressure Wash LLC. By authorizing a connection, you confirm that you have authority to do so for the connected MPW business account.

Purpose and limits

The bridge compares completed Jobber work with QuickBooks Online invoice records to produce internal reconciliation exceptions. It is not bookkeeping, tax, CPA, legal, or financial advice and does not replace human review by MPW.

Authorized access and read-only design

Only authorized MPW personnel may use the bridge. Users must protect credentials and review exceptions before acting. The bridge queries information only and does not create, edit, send, void, delete, pay, or collect on a QuickBooks transaction. MPW retains final authority over all accounting and operational decisions.

MPW may update these terms by posting a revised version here. Questions: info@misterpressurewash.com.

Connect or Reconnect

MPW Codex Finance Bridge is an internal MPW tool. An authorized MPW administrator initiates a connection through the secure administrator workflow. This public page does not collect QuickBooks credentials or create financial transactions.

For authorized connection assistance, contact info@misterpressurewash.com.

Disconnect

An authorized MPW administrator may disconnect the bridge and request deletion of its encrypted authorization tokens and retained reconciliation reports. No QuickBooks record is changed by disconnection.

To request a disconnection, contact info@misterpressurewash.com.
